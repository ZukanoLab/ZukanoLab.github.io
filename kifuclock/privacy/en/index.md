# KifuClock Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026

Last updated: November 4, 2026

ZukanoLab ("we", "us", or "our") provides the app "KifuClock" (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 1. Information We Collect and Transmit

The App does not provide account registration or require a name, email address, or phone number to register. Player names entered in game records or history are stored on your device and are not sent to the analytics or purchase management services.

If you agree to share usage data, we transmit the following information with a randomly generated identifier to an analytics service to improve the App. The identifier does not directly reveal your name or email address, but it allows actions using the same identifier to be analyzed together:

- A random anonymous identifier generated by the analytics SDK
- Usage events such as app sessions, game starts and completions, time graph views, and kifu creation, import, and linking
- Pro and support screen views, support product availability, purchase starts, completions, cancellations, pending payments and failures, and purchase restoration events, including product scope, support tier, outcome, Pro entitlement status, and the number of restored entitlements
- Aggregate properties such as timer mode, move count, game duration, and board size
- Technical information such as operating system and version, device model, manufacturer and type, screen size, app version and build number, display language, device language, time zone, emulator status, event timestamps, and session information

To provide in-app purchases, purchase restoration, and purchased features, the App transmits the following information to a purchase management service:

- A random anonymous identifier generated by the purchase management SDK
- Product, purchase status, purchase history, and entitlement status
- Information needed to validate purchases, such as an Apple purchase receipt or Google Play purchase token
- Technical information such as operating system, device type, and app version

The App has no feature that links these anonymous identifiers to a login account, name, email address, player name, or similar identifying information. Payment card and bank account information is handled by Apple or Google and is not collected by us.

The App does not send player names, kifu or SGF contents, SGF file names, the game history itself, free-form text, contacts, photos, videos, audio, advertising identifiers, or precise device location to the analytics or purchase management services.

For communications related to checking for new versions, see GitHub Pages in Section 4. For information we receive when you contact us, see Section 11.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

- Timer settings
- Favorite presets
- Display language settings
- Spoken countdown, turn sound, vibration, and keep-awake settings
- Game history and kifu
- Anonymous analytics preference

We do not operate our own server to store or synchronize this app data. If you share or export data, it is passed to the destination you select. The usage information and purchase management information described in Section 1 are transmitted to the applicable services. Device backups provided by the operating system or enabled in your settings are governed by the relevant operating system or backup service.

## 3. Speech, Sound, and Vibration

The App uses your device's text-to-speech, audio playback, and vibration features to notify you about countdowns and time-up events.

The App does not record your voice or access microphone input.

## 4. Third-Party Services

When the App communicates with these services, the relevant service provider receives connection information, such as the source IP address. Information may be processed on servers outside Japan by these services.

### PostHog

The App uses PostHog, an analytics service provided by PostHog, Inc., to understand usage and improve the App.

PostHog groups actions by the same user using a random identifier. We do not link it to a login account, name, email address, or player name. The App does not use IP-based geolocation enrichment, advertising identifiers, or session recordings. Analytics can be stopped in Advanced Settings.

For details about how PostHog handles information, see the [PostHog Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

### RevenueCat

The App uses RevenueCat, a purchase management service provided by RevenueCat, Inc., to retrieve product information, process in-app purchases, restore purchases, validate purchase receipts, and manage access to purchased features.

The App does not provide RevenueCat with a custom user ID. It uses a random anonymous identifier generated by RevenueCat. To provide its service, RevenueCat processes technical device information, purchase receipts or purchase tokens, purchase history, and entitlement status.

Purchase management information is also used to summarize purchases and revenue. This processing is separate from sharing usage data with PostHog and does not stop when usage sharing is turned off.

For details about how RevenueCat handles information, see the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy-policy).

### GitHub Pages

We use GitHub Pages to deliver update information. To protect the hosting service, GitHub records and stores connection information (IP addresses). The App does not attach kifu, player names, or analytics or purchase management identifiers to update-check requests.

Our public support page and privacy policy also use GitHub Pages. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

### Apple and Google

In-app payment transactions are processed by Apple on iOS and Google Play on Android. For details about their handling of information, see the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Purposes of Use

We use the information described above to:

- Provide the App, improve quality, and investigate issues
- Analyze aggregate usage and improve features
- Display products, process purchases, restore purchases, and provide purchased features
- Summarize purchases and revenue
- Validate purchases, prevent fraud, and respond to purchase-related support requests
- Provide update notices and public pages, and support the security of the services the App communicates with

## 6. Sharing of Information

We may use information collected through the App, after aggregating and anonymizing it so that no individual or device can be identified, for feature improvement, usage trend analysis, research, or public reporting, and may provide such information to third parties.

We do not sell information that could identify an individual or device, or use or provide such information for third-party advertising or user tracking.

Information is transmitted to PostHog, RevenueCat, GitHub, Apple, or Google only as needed to provide the services described in Section 4. We may also disclose information to the extent required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.

## 7. Security

Information transmitted by the App to PostHog, RevenueCat, GitHub, Apple, or Google is protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS. App data stored on the device is kept in the app-specific storage provided by the operating system.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. These measures include limiting services and permissions to what is necessary and not storing credentials in public repositories. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

## 8. Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

We store usage information in PostHog to improve the App and analyze long-term usage trends. Storage and availability are governed by our PostHog plan and its retention terms. This does not guarantee that all data will be completely deleted when the retention period has elapsed. See [PostHog event retention terms](https://posthog.com/docs/data/events-retention).

We retain purchase management information for as long as needed to provide and restore purchased features, prevent fraud, and fulfill related purposes. We retain support information for as long as needed to respond, resolve issues, and provide follow-up assistance. Information that must be retained for legal obligations or transaction records is kept for the required period. Information independently retained by service providers is governed by their respective policies.

Settings, game history, kifu, and favorite presets stored on your device can be deleted using the corresponding delete or reset features. Exported files, operating-system backups, and information already transmitted to services may remain separately after the App is deleted. A new anonymous identifier may be generated if the App is reinstalled.

You can choose whether to share usage data on the first launch. Analytics events are not sent before you choose to share. You can stop sharing at any time in Advanced Settings, which stops future usage-event transmission to PostHog. Stopping sharing does not delete analytics data already sent. This choice does not stop processing by RevenueCat, Apple, or Google that is needed for purchases and restoration, or update-check requests to GitHub Pages.

Analytics data is stored under a random identifier that we do not link to names or email addresses. The App does not provide features for viewing this identifier, sending it to us, or individually deleting analytics data that has already been transmitted. We therefore do not provide individual analytics-data identification and deletion in response to inquiries.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App does not require users, including children, to enter a name, email address, or phone number to register. Player names stored on the device are described in Section 1. Parents and guardians may also contact us through Section 11 about information handling. Retention and deletion are described in Section 8.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we will notify users in the App, through app store listing information, or by similar means.
